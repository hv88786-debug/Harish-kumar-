I'm a first-year B.Tech CSE student at Government Engineering College, Ajmer. I like picking real, unglamorous problems — mostly around healthcare, agriculture, and water access in Rajasthan — and checking whether I can actually build something that works for them.

Most of what I build sits across AI, IoT, and full-stack web: an ESP32 reading sensor data, a Flask or Node backend making sense of it, an LLM deciding what to do next.
