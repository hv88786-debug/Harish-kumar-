Approach

Engineering Principles

Build with purpose. Every project begins by understanding the problem, identifying constraints, and exploring possible solutions before writing code.

Document everything. Research, design decisions, development progress, GitHub commits, and video walkthroughs are documented as part of the development process.

Learn continuously. Hackathons, Smart India Hackathon problem statements, and personal projects are opportunities to experiment with new technologies and improve problem-solving skills.

Iterate relentlessly. Every project is reviewed after completion. Feedback, testing, and real-world usage help shape the next version.

About
Computer Science Student

I'm a second-year B.Tech CSE student at Government Engineering College, Ajmer. I like picking real, unglamorous problems — mostly around healthcare, agriculture, and water access in Rajasthan — and checking whether I can actually build something that works for them.

Most of what I build sits across AI, IoT, and full-stack web: an ESP32 reading sensor data, a Flask or Node backend making sense of it, an LLM deciding what to do next — wrapped in something that still works for someone with patchy internet and no budget for expensive hardware.

I also try to build in public. For 30 straight days I designed, coded, and documented a new SIH-style solution every day — mostly to force myself to ship instead of overthink. Outside of that, I run Team Alpha Coders with Lucky Gupta and Jiya Sapnani, and we've carried the same "ship it, then fix it" habit into hackathons at state and national level.

KRISHI-AI — Smart Farming Platform

Full-stack smart farming ecosystem for Indian farmers — combining ESP32 IoT sensors, CNN-based crop disease detection, live mandi prices, and government scheme matching.

  • ESP32 + DHT11 sensors monitoring soil moisture, temperature and humidity with auto pump control
  • CNN model on TensorFlow detecting 38 crop diseases with offline-first capability
  • Live market prices from Rajasthan mandis with AI price-trend analysis
  • Auto-matches farmer profiles to central and state government schemes (PM Kisan, Fasal Bima)

AquaGuard — Water Quality Monitoring System

Real-time IoT water quality monitoring system targeting rural Rajasthan villages under Jal Jeevan Mission. ESP32-based sensor node with AI-powered disease outbreak risk prediction.

  • Measures TDS, turbidity, temperature and salinity in real-time using ESP32 sensor array
  • DIY flexible salinity sensor built for ₹2 (replacing a ₹2000 market equivalent) — 100× cost reduction
  • Groq AI (LLaMA 3.3-70B) backend for disease outbreak risk prediction and WhatsApp alerts
  • Firebase Realtime Database with bilingual Hindi + English government-style dashboard

Smart Healthcare Navigation System

Offline-first mobile app for Jawaharlal Nehru Hospital, Ajmer — guiding rural patients from hospital gate to correct department without internet. Paired with an AI voice agent for keypad phone users.

  • Fully offline navigation with illness-to-department mapping (only 10% network coverage at JLN)
  • Marwadi language support for rural patient accessibility
  • AI Voice Agent (call-based) covering non-smartphone users — estimated 55% of patient volume
